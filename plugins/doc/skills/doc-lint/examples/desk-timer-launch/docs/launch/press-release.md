# Tomo Timer: a one-knob desk timer that tells you when to take a break

Tomo Timer is a palm-sized desk timer you set without looking at a screen. Pre-orders open on 2026-11-02 at the Tomo Timer online shop for 4,980 yen including tax.

Set a timer in one twist. Turn the knob to choose from 1 to 90 minutes, press it, and a 12-LED ring counts down. When time is up, Tomo Timer chimes once and glows amber until you press the knob again. It needs no app and no account, and it runs from any USB-C charger.

"I wanted something my grandmother could use without instructions," says the maker.

## About Tomo Timer

Tomo Timer is for desk workers who forget to take breaks and for students who time focus sessions. It is 62 mm across and 28 mm tall.
